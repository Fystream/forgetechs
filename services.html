01 Web design

Designed around the one thing you need a visitor to do.

Before any layout, we settle what the site is for — a booking, a call, directions, a price list. Everything else arranges itself around that. You get a design a first-time visitor understands in seconds, on the phone they actually own.

  • Custom layout for every project, never a theme
  • Type, colour and spacing chosen for your trade
  • Designed mobile-first, checked on real low-end devices
  • Multilingual content handled properly
02 Development

Built properly, so it stays fast and stays yours.

Built lean, with nothing quietly rotting in the background and nothing you have to keep updating. It opens quickly on a weak mobile connection anywhere in the world, and it cannot break because someone else’s plugin changed.

  • Lightweight front-end code, no generated bloat
  • Works on slow and intermittent connections
  • WhatsApp, call, maps and booking links wired in
  • You own every file — no licence, no lock-in
03 Local SEO

Found by the people who are already nearby.

Most of your customers are searching from within a few kilometres. That is a different job from ranking nationally, and it is a job that is genuinely winnable.

  • Google Business Profile set up or cleaned up
  • Schema.org structured data on every page
  • Keywords for your town, not the country
  • Submitted and indexed before handover

04 Upkeep

Changes when you need them. No monthly retainer.

Prices change, timings change, you add a service. Message me and it is done — usually the same day. I do not put small businesses on a subscription for work that takes ten minutes.

  • Anything broken, fixed free — it was my code

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

Four steps, in order. You’re only ever waiting on one thing at a time, and you always know which one.

01 Step one · 15 minutes

Brief

A call or a chat. What your business does, who walks through the door, and what the site has to make happen. I ask the awkward questions now so there are no surprises later.

02 Step two

Design

I build a real page, not a mockup. You get a live link you can open on your phone and show to anyone whose opinion you trust.
